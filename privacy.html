1. Who We Are

Personal Code is operated by , based in . You can reach us at for any questions regarding your information.

2. Who May Use the Service

Personal Code is for people aged 18 or older. Before you generate a reading, pay, or send us a message, we ask you to tick a box confirming you are 18 or older. We do not check ages or ask for identity documents. If you are under 18, please do not use the service. If we learn that someone under 18 has used it, we will delete what we hold about them.

3. What Stays on Your Device

The name, date of birth, birth time, and birthplace you type to build a reading are used inside your browser to work out your reading. We do not receive or store them, with one exception: if you enter a birthplace, its text is sent to our server, which asks OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service for its coordinates. Nothing else you entered is sent. We cache the place text and its coordinates for up to 30 days to reduce repeat lookups. The PDF you download is created on your device.

4. What We and Our Providers Collect

We collect minimal personal data necessary to process transactions, fulfil orders, and maintain a secure platform:

  • Payment Processing: All payments are processed securely through our payment provider, Paystack. Paystack collects billing details, contact information, and transaction data directly under their own privacy policy. We receive transaction confirmations to give you access, handle customer support, execute refunds, and maintain accounting records, but we never store raw payment credentials on our servers.
  • User Feedback: If you send us feedback or suggestions, we collect your message, feedback category, optional name and contact details, whether you allow public display, and the submission time, to evaluate your input and respond where appropriate.
  • Site Security & Infrastructure: Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard server logs (including IP addresses) to deliver pages reliably and protect the site from abuse. For the same purpose, short-lived request counters tied to your IP address are kept for up to 10 minutes to limit repeated requests.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not set tracking cookies, and we do not load fonts or scripts from third-party servers. We do not sell your information.

5. Who We Share It With

We share minimal data with trusted third-party service providers to run our platform: Paystack (payment processing), Cloudflare (hosting and security), and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (birthplace coordinate lookup). These providers process data in accordance with their respective privacy standards and applicable international laws. We only disclose information further if required by law or to protect the service from misuse.

6. How Long We Keep It

  • Payment Records: Retained in our Paystack merchant dashboard as long as necessary for support, accounting, and legal compliance.
  • Feedback Submissions: Retained until you request deletion or until we clean up stored records.
  • Birthplace Lookups: Cached for up to 30 days to optimise search performance.
  • Request Counters: Kept for up to 10 minutes.

7. Your Choices

You may email us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask to delete a submitted feedback message. Paystack holds its own transaction records, and you may contact Paystack directly regarding those details. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may also contact the data protection authority in your country.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, uses security headers, keeps payment keys securely on the server (out of the browser), and restricts administrative access to user messages. No online service can promise perfect security, so please avoid including private or sensitive personal details in general feedback messages.

9. Not Advice

Readings are symbolic and meant for reflection and entertainment. They do not constitute medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
